## The project

Studio Aperto is a Milanese architecture studio that needed an identity capable of telling its philosophy: spaces that breathe, forms that dialogue with those who inhabit them.

The brief called for simplicity and solidity, without slipping into sterile minimalism.

## The process

We started from a sector analysis and a series of conversations with the team. A strong typographic direction emerged, built around a serif logotype with reduced spacing.

The colour system is based on three colours: a warm white, a slate grey and a discreet sage green for accents.

## The result

The new system was applied to business cards, letterhead, presentations and social profiles. The client's response was positive: "we finally see ourselves as we want to be seen."
