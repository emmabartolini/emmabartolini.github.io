## The context

Liceo Artistico Brera was looking for a learning experience that would help students understand how the design process works: not as a set of aesthetic rules, but as a method of thinking.

We built a three-day journey that started from a real problem (how to improve the school's common spaces?) and arrived at concrete visual and narrative proposals.

## The structure

**Day 1 — Observe:** active observation exercises, user interviews, problem mapping.

**Day 2 — Imagine:** ideation, rapid sketching, paper prototyping.

**Day 3 — Narrate:** how to present an idea, how to build a visual narrative.

## What remained

At the end of the workshop, each group had produced a small intervention proposal for the school. Some ideas were actually later collected by the management.

The best part: watching 17-year-olds discover that design isn't "making beautiful things" but "solving problems with intention."
