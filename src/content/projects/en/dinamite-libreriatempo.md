I curated and built the project from start to finish: from art direction to graphic design, from setting up the physical space to producing events, from writing materials to education outside schools as a practice. A physical, temporary space where self-productions stood alongside meetings with authors, with the aim of creating an environment free from external influences, where those who entered could reflect, discuss and act.

dinamite – libreriatempo is temporary by definition and by choice. It is still looking for new spaces where it can shine.
